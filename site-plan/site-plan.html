Moquegua Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

Site Name & Purpose

Site Name: Moquegua Chamber of Commerce

Purpose: This website will serve as a resource hub for businesses in the city of Moquegua. The site will provide information on local events, host networking opportunities, create business directories, and encourage local shopping to promote local economic growth.

Target Market: Business owners, patrons, and residents in the city of Moquegua and surrounding areas.

Site Goals

  • Improve member engagement by providing valuable resources and networking opportunities.
  • Attract new businesses and encourage economic development in the community.
  • Improve the visibility and reputation of the chamber as a trusted authority in local business matters.

User Personas & Scenarios

1. Small Business Owner

Persona: Maria is a 35-year-old entrepreneur who owns a small boutique shop in town. She is looking for networking opportunities, business resources, and marketing support to help grow her business.

Scenario: Maria wants to join the chamber of commerce to network. She visits the website to find information on membership benefits, fees, and fills out the application form on the Join page.

2. Corporate Executive

Persona: Diego is a 47-year-old executive at a large company looking for growth opportunities, local partnerships, and networking with other business leaders.

Scenario: Diego visits the website to browse the Directory to identify potential corporate partners and check upcoming chamber events.

3. The New Resident

Persona: Francisco recently moved into Moquegua and wants to learn more about services, local events, and community activities.

Scenario: Francisco visits the Discover page to check local demographics, history, and community events, then searches the Directory for local service providers.

SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords in site descriptions, content, and metadata for Moquegua business searches.
  • Verify the site on Google's Business Profile.
  • Get inbound links or backlinks from member business sites.
  • Embed Google Analytics across all site pages to track visitor behavior.

Design Schema

Primary Color: #000000 (Black - Header & Footer)

Secondary Color: #FFD700 (Gold - Accents & Hover State)

Background Color: #F5F5DC (Beige - Card Container Backgrounds)

Link Color: #1B4965 (Deep Blue - Navigation & Hyperlinks)

Typography: 'Poppins' (Headings) and 'Roboto' (Body text)
